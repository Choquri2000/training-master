Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Combat Coach helps you learn combat sports techniques. This policy explains what data the app keeps and why.

What we collect

Your training data: chosen skills, progress, ratings, logged sessions, notes and settings. If you create an account, your email address and a sign-in ID from Google or Firebase. If you subscribe, our reseller Paddle tells us your subscription status, plan and renewal date. Paddle processes your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details.

Where it is stored

On your device. If you sign in, also in Google Firebase (servers in the EU), so you can use the app on more than one device. Only you can read your saved data.

Who processes your data

Google Firebase (sign-in and storage), Cloudflare (checks payment messages from Paddle), and Paddle (payments). Each only gets what its job needs.

What we do not do

No ads, no selling or sharing of your data, no tracking across other apps or sites. Voice commands are processed by your phone or browser, and the app does not record or upload audio.

Your rights

You can export your data at any time (Me → Your data → Export). You can delete your account, cloud copy and subscription record in the app or on the website (sign in at choquri2000.github.io/training-master, then Me → Settings → Delete account), or by contacting us. Deleting cancels any subscription. Deletion is permanent; Paddle keeps billing records as tax law requires.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/Choquri2000/training-master.
